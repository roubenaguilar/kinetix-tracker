How the ±4.5° Last-Two-MA Slope Works:

We evaluate strictly the last two Moving Average values (MA[n-1] and MA[n]) in the active window. Normalizing the delta by the window's metric scale gives angle θ = atan2(Δy_norm, Δx_norm) × (180/π). Angles within [-4.5°, +4.5°] indicate No Change.
